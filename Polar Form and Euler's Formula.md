

> [!info] Intro
> Times any complex number is equivalent to stretch and then rotate in the complex plane.


Introduce polar coordinates:

$$
a=r\cos\theta,
\qquad
b=r\sin\theta.
$$

Then

$$
a+bi
=
r(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta).
$$

Euler’s identity (shown by Taylor's series) states that

$$
e^{i\theta}
=
\cos\theta+i\sin\theta.
$$

Hence every complex number can be written as

$$
z=re^{i\theta}.
$$

This decomposes a complex number into two geometrically distinct actions:

$$
re^{i\theta}
=
(\text{scaling by }r)
\times
(\text{rotation by }\theta).
$$

Complex multiplication therefore becomes

$$
r_1e^{i\theta_1}\cdot r_2e^{i\theta_2}
=
r_1r_2e^{i(\theta_1+\theta_2)}.
$$

The magnitudes multiply while the angles add.

Thus multiplication by a complex number means:

> stretch, then rotate. *vice versa*.
